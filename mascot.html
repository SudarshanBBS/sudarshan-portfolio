ARCHITECTURE & INTERACTION

What Buddy does all day

Buddy Bot bridges high-performance WebGL graphics with dynamic conversational UX. Built without any 3D asset downloads, heavy textures, or bloated dependencies.

🎭

Live Canvas Face Screen

Every eye movement, blink, smile, and wink is painted in real time onto an internal HTML canvas texture. Crisp at any retina scale with zero texture lag.

⚡

Reaction State Machine

Buddy transitions between realistic kinetic physics: squash-and-stretch jumps, affirmative nods, friendly waving hands, and 360° pirouette spins.

👁️

Cursor Gaze Tracking

Smooth damped interpolation keeps Buddy's head and digital pupils focused on your cursor position across the entire viewport.

🎨

Live Color Themes

Four calibrated palettes (Crimson, Cobalt, Lime, Grape) harmonize the WebGL materials, ear-glow rings, and page typography in real time.
